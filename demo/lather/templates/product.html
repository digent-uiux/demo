Keep the showersimple.

Soft foam. A thorough rinse. Then a little lotion while skin is still damp.

  1. Wet, then lather

    Work a small amount between wet palms. Wash gently with lukewarm water.

  2. Rinse it away

    Rinse the foam off fully. Skip the hard scrubbing and pat dry with a soft towel.

  3. Follow with lotion

    Keep Calm lotion nearby. Smooth it over damp skin after your shower.

New to your skin? Try a small area first. Stop using if irritation occurs.

All help and answers
Does fragrance-free mean it has no smell?

We add no perfume or essential oils to Calm wash. You may notice a faint scent from the ingredients themselves.

Can I use it if I have an oat allergy?

Calm wash contains colloidal oat. Check the full ingredient list against your known allergies, and ask your healthcare professional if you are unsure.

How is Rich wash different?

Rich wash has extra shea butter and oat oil, with a thicker, almost lotion-like feel. Calm wash is the everyday starting point. See Rich wash

What if my skin reacts?

Stop using it and contact us. We refund your first bottle, used or not. Read the skin-reaction refund details.
