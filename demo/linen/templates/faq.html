Help · 14 answers on size, returns and care

Questions, kindly answered

Linen asks a little more of you than cotton, and gives a lot back. Here's what people ask us before and after their first piece.

Size · 3 questions

Finding your fit

  • Most are cut relaxed on purpose, with room to move in the heat. If you like a closer line, size down; each product page says how the model wears it.

    Was this helpful?

  • Take the smaller one in dresses and shirts, the larger one in trousers. Linen gives a little across the hips after a few wears but never shrinks back.

    Was this helpful?

  • Under the size selector on every product: bust, waist, hip and length for each size, measured flat on the finished garment.

    Was this helpful?

Size · 2 questions

Lengths and petite

  • Trousers come in short (74 cm inside leg), regular (79 cm) and long (84 cm). Dresses are one length; free hemming is offered on the product page.

    Was this helpful?

  • It ends 20 cm below the knee on someone 168 cm tall. Every 5 cm of height moves it about 3 cm.

    Was this helpful?

Returns · 3 questions

Sending it back

  • Thirty days from delivery, unworn and unwashed, with the paper tag still on. Returns in the US are free with the label in your parcel.

    Was this helpful?

  • Not for a refund, but if it shrank or lost its shape after one wash on our care instructions, we replace it.

    Was this helpful?

  • Within three working days of your parcel reaching our studio. You get an email the moment we scan it.

    Was this helpful?

Returns · 2 questions

Exchanges

  • Yes, free both ways. Start the swap in your account and we ship the new size as soon as yours is on its way back.

    Was this helpful?

  • Pick “notify me” on the product page. We cut small runs every six weeks and email you first when it's back.

    Was this helpful?

Washing · 2 questions

Washing linen

  • Yes, at 30°C on a gentle cycle, inside out, with a mild liquid detergent. Skip fabric softener: it coats the fibres and stops linen softening on its own.

    Was this helpful?

  • Better not. Dry it on a line or a hanger in the shade; ten minutes in the dryer on low before hanging is the most we'd risk.

    Was this helpful?

Washing · 2 questions

Creases and softness

  • Hang it while still damp and smooth the seams by hand. If you iron, do it slightly damp on a medium setting, inside out. A few soft creases are part of the look.

    Was this helpful?

  • Yes. Every piece is stone-washed before it reaches you, and it keeps softening for the first ten or so washes.

    Was this helpful?
