Washing · 2 questions

Washing linen

  • Yes, at 30°C on a gentle cycle, inside out, with a mild liquid detergent. Skip fabric softener: it coats the fibres and stops linen softening on its own.

    Was this helpful?

  • Better not. Dry it on a line or a hanger in the shade; ten minutes in the dryer on low before hanging is the most we'd risk.

    Was this helpful?

Washing · 2 questions

Creases and softness

  • Hang it while still damp and smooth the seams by hand. If you iron, do it slightly damp on a medium setting, inside out. A few soft creases are part of the look.

    Was this helpful?

  • Yes. Every piece is stone-washed before it reaches you, and it keeps softening for the first ten or so washes.
