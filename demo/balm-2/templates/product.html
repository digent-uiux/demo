How it’s made

Four steps, one small batch

Every bottle starts as herbs in oil at our workshop bench, and ships four weeks later.

Printed on the bottle’s label.

Dates show a typical batch.

  1. No. 01Day 0–21

    Macerate

    Dried calendula and rosehip steep in cold-pressed oil for three weeks, turned by hand each morning.

  2. No. 02Day 22

    Press

    The herbs are pressed out through linen and the golden oil rests overnight.

  3. No. 03Day 23

    Pour

    Oils go into amber glass, balms and bars into tins and moulds, all by hand.

  4. No. 04Day 24

    Label

    Each piece gets a paper label with its batch code, then rests a few days before it ships.

  5. Ships · Day 28
